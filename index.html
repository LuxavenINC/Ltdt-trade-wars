        World Assembly

        Every 4 years the presidents vote to sanction a hostile country, one that attacks others. Peaceful countries building their economy are left alone. The sanctioned country earns half its income, cannot buy gold and must not attack anyone until the next session, or every country goes to war with it. Presidents who vote receive 5,000 gold bars for protecting the peace and rebuilding the economy; those who do not vote receive nothing, and the sanctioned one only 1,000. If no one votes, every country receives just 200.

        Every nation's foreign exchange reserves, in world credits. Held abroad is how much of that currency other nations hold. Dump risk is how far it would fall if its biggest holder dumped everything at once, after its city shield. Watch your own row: a big foreign holder can crash your currency in one move.

        World prices in each nation's own money

        Prices are in each nation's own currency, so a weak currency shows high prices and a strong one low prices. WC is what 1 unit of that currency is worth in world credits, the same rate as the Currency markets chart; Gold is a 1 oz bar. Food gets dearer with poverty, empty granaries and famine; a gold bar and oil follow the gold price.
